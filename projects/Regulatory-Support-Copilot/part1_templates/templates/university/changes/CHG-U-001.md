# Eight-week internship for computing programs

## Requirements

1. From 1 September 2027, CS-BSc and SE-BSc students must complete an internship of 8 weeks instead of 6 weeks.
2. The CS-BSc and SE-BSc study plans must show the new duration and placement assessment.
3. Program coordinators must communicate the requirement to computing cohorts and international students enrolled in those programs.

## Background

The existing internship lasts six weeks.
