# Maximum absence reduced to ten percent

## Requirements

1. From 1 September 2026, CS-BSc, SE-BSc, BUS-BBA and NUR-BSc must apply a maximum absence of 10% instead of 15%.
2. The four programs must update absence warnings and notify all student groups, including international students.
3. Academic advisors supporting the four programs must review students approaching the new absence limit.

## Background

The present attendance policy permits absence up to fifteen percent.
