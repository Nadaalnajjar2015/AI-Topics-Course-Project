# English proficiency certificate for international students

## Requirements

1. From 1 September 2026, INTL-OFF must verify an English proficiency certificate for international students.
2. INTL-OFF must record whether the certificate has been verified before admission clearance.
3. INTL-OFF must notify international applicants about missing certificates and the review route.

## Background

International applicants follow several different admission pathways.
