## Registration and advisor approval

NUR210 requires NUR101. Missing that prerequisite produces PREREQ_MISSING. An outstanding advisor approval is a separate ADVISOR_HOLD; completing a prerequisite does not automatically clear an advisor hold.

Supervised practice sections have limited capacity. SECTION_FULL means waitlisted, not registered; an advisor can help choose an available section. Fees, timetable conflicts, the normal 18-credit limit and the 12 February 2026 17:00 registration deadline follow registration_rules.md. Only a REGISTERED record confirms successful registration.

## Internship

The fictional baseline placement lasts 6 weeks and requires advisor coordination and a supervisor's completion report, as described in internship_guide.md. CHG-U-001 changes only CS-BSc and SE-BSc; it does not extend the nursing placement to 8 weeks.

## Attendance

At the February 2026 request dates, the maximum absence is 15% of scheduled teaching sessions. CHG-U-003 includes NUR-BSc and reduces that limit to 10% from 1 September 2026. Refer to attendance_policy.md and the dated change when answering later questions.

## Graduation requirements

Complete the approved program plan, pass required prerequisites and the placement, obtain the completion report, and clear academic and fees holds. This excerpt does not define a full degree credit total.
