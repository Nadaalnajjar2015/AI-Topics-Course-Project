# Registration rules

## Credit limits

The normal maximum is 18 credits per semester. Exceeding the limit requires academic approval.

## Registration deadlines

The registration week runs from 8 February through 12 February 2026. The standard deadline is 12 February at 17:00; subsequent attempts require registrar approval.

## Dropping a course

A student may request a drop through the Registrar's Office after discussing study-plan progress with an advisor.

## PREREQ_MISSING

Meaning: Prerequisite missing.

Typical cause: A required prerequisite has not been passed.

What the student should do: Check the study plan and complete the prerequisite; SE301 requires SE201.

Who can help: Academic advisor.

## SECTION_FULL

Meaning: Section full.

Typical cause: The section has reached its capacity.

What the student should do: Join the waiting list or choose an available section.

Who can help: Registrar's Office.

## FEES_UNPAID

Meaning: Fees unpaid.

Typical cause: An unpaid balance has placed a registration hold.

What the student should do: Resolve the balance and ask for the hold to be cleared.

Who can help: Fees office.

## TIME_CONFLICT

Meaning: Time conflict.

Typical cause: The chosen section overlaps an existing registration.

What the student should do: Choose a section with a different timetable.

Who can help: Academic advisor.

## CREDIT_LIMIT

Meaning: Credit limit exceeded.

Typical cause: The new course would exceed 18 credits.

What the student should do: Review the course load with an advisor.

Who can help: Academic advisor.

## ADVISOR_HOLD

Meaning: Advisor hold.

Typical cause: Academic approval has not been recorded.

What the student should do: Meet the advisor and request approval.

Who can help: Academic advisor.

## LATE_REGISTRATION

Meaning: Late registration.

Typical cause: The registration request arrived after the deadline.

What the student should do: Ask the registrar about the late-registration process.

Who can help: Registrar's Office.
