# Earlier cheque cut-off time

## Requirements

1. From 15 May 2026, CHQ participants in Country Y must close same-day cheque submission at 13:00.
2. CHQ must return late submissions with TM01 and show the next eligible processing window.
3. CHQ operators must test submissions immediately before and after 13:00.

## Background

The previous Country Y profile allowed submissions until 15:00.
